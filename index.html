About me

I am a 21-year-old game designer based in Barcelona, Spain, who loves turning ideas of any kind into clean, polished, and, most importantly, fun gameplay experiences! My main engine is GameMaker Studio 2, but I also have experience in Unity!

I've been working as a game designer for the past five years while I finish my Computer Science degree. Although I don't have any released games on the market yet, I've worked with several indie teams through freelance commissions, which has helped me improve my skills and gain valuable experience.

My main goal is to grow within the game industry, work on exciting and meaningful projects, keep learning from talented developers along the way, and hopefully launch my own project.
